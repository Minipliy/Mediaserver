Centered simple bold geometric symbol combining a white play triangle with two cyan media-library layers behind it, highly legible at small size. Restrained polished flat vector-like appearance, generous inner spacing, no text, no letters, no watermark, no rounded outer corners, no device mockup, no border, no transparent area. The image itself is the complete app icon ready for an iOS asset catalog.
